## What this is

The OSO P.I.T. is where the pit first got its name. In September 2025, at
**ETHGlobal New Delhi**, a small crew built the Open Source Orchestra's pit:
a livestreaming platform where the stage is a **visual orchestra pit** —
musicians queue at golden stands to perform, listeners gift them directly with
**zero platform fees**, and every artist receives an ENS name issued on L2
through the Durin architecture. The judges named it
**🏆 Most Creative Use of ENS**.

A year later the same three letters — P.I.T. — deepened into
**Public Information Transmission**, the protocol of Knowledge Transponders
that keeps a village's signal on the air forever. This repository is the
living door of that pit, and the preserved tomb of its ancestor.
